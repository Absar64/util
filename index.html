Address your phone uses

Every address on this page is built from this, so change it here and the shortcut URLs, the care links and the tag address all follow. A 192.168.x.x address only works on your home WiFi - shortcuts will fail on mobile data until this points at something reachable from outside.

What he tracks

The name is the whole definition: it is read as plain English and works out what it means. A parameter that never fires still counts as news - "Left from home: has not happened at all today" is something he can use.

Automations

Each rule watches one event from your iOS Shortcuts. interrupt cuts in immediately, queue waits for the current script, notify pulses until you press a button, and weave says nothing now but feeds the fact into the next generated script.
